## The model

Print-on-demand, zero inventory, 72-hour drops that never restock. Same economics as the reference site;
different market, speed and brand.

## COD policy

Indian COD return-to-origin rates commonly run 15–30%, and a POD order cannot be cancelled once printed —
every RTO is a total loss of production plus two-way shipping.

v1 policy:

1. **First order is prepaid only.** COD unlocks after one delivered prepaid order.
2. **COD carries a ₹80 handling fee**, shown before the order is placed.
3. **COD orders get a confirmation step** (WhatsApp or OTP) before submission to the printer.
4. The admin tracks COD share and RTO rate per drop; if RTO exceeds 20%, COD is switched off per drop.

## Drop cadence

One drop every 1–2 weeks to start, 72 hours each. Faster than that dilutes the countdown; slower and the
trend is gone. Edition size optional — an edition cap is a stronger scarcity signal than a clock alone.

## IP rules — non-negotiable

Trending is not the same as free to use. A takedown after printing is unrecoverable.

- No logos, trademarks or brand names.
- No characters, likenesses, or real people's names or faces.
- No song lyrics, film quotes or other copyrighted text.
- Original typographic and illustrative commentary only.
- If it is arguable, it is a no. Record the reasoning on the drop.

## What to measure

Conversion rate per drop, AOV, prepaid vs COD share, RTO rate, print SLA, and spotted-to-live time.
The last one is the business's actual moat.
